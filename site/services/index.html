Our services

The right build for your next step.

A fresh start, a better version of what you have, or a tool that makes the day easier. We help you choose a sensible scope for your needs and budget.

  1. 01

    Custom websites

    A website built around your business, your customers and the action you want them to take. From a focused landing page to a complete small-business website.

    • Brand-led design that works across phones, tablets and desktops
    • Services, menus, projects and contact information organised clearly
    • Content editing and search basics planned into the build

    New businesses, local shops, independent professionals

  2. 02

    Website redesigns & upgrades

    Keep what works and improve what gets in the way. We review your existing website before recommending a focused update or a fuller redesign.

    • Clearer mobile layouts, navigation and enquiry paths
    • Refreshed content, visuals and page structure
    • Migration and existing page addresses considered before launch

    Established businesses, rebrands, outgrown websites

  3. 03

    Bookings, ordering & online shops

    Make it easier for customers to book, enquire or buy. We connect suitable, established services and shape the website around the way you already run your business.

    • Appointments, table bookings and enquiry forms
    • Product catalogues, menus and hosted checkout connections
    • Tool compatibility and ongoing platform fees agreed upfront

    Salons, restaurants, classes, small retailers

  4. 04

    Custom apps & prototypes

    A focused app for a specific business need. We begin with the customer journey and a testable prototype, then agree the first version and the best platform to build on.

    • Customer apps, membership journeys and booking experiences
    • iOS, Android or a web app, chosen after a feasibility review
    • Development, testing, release support and maintenance scoped separately

    Businesses with repeat-use needs, new product ideas

  5. 05

    Web apps & business tools

    Bring a repetitive process into one practical workspace. We can plan and build focused tools for your team, or connect systems that currently leave you doing the same work twice.

    • Customer portals, quote tools and internal dashboards
    • Forms, data workflows and integrations with existing services
    • Access, data handling and handover planned from the start

    Service teams, growing businesses, manual workflows

  6. 06

    Bilingual websites & ongoing care

    Keep your website useful as your business changes. We help with English and Chinese content, updates and the practical work of looking after a live site.

    • English and Chinese page structure and content support
    • Hosting setup, content updates and agreed maintenance
    • Account ownership, editing guidance and a clear handover

    Bilingual businesses, existing websites, ongoing support

Our approach to pricing

Fair pricing. Clearly scoped.

We shape the proposal around what your business actually needs and what you are comfortable investing. Every project is quoted individually, with the costs explained before you commit.

  1. 01

    A written quote before work starts

    You can see what is included, what will be delivered and how payments are staged. The agreed scope sets the price.

  2. 02

    A sensible first version

    We can prioritise the essentials and plan the rest for later. A smaller, useful launch is often a good place to begin.

  3. 03

    Ongoing costs made clear

    Hosting, domains, third-party subscriptions, maintenance and any applicable taxes are identified in the proposal.

  4. 04

    Changes agreed together

    If the brief changes, we explain the effect on cost and timing and get your agreement before doing additional work.

A few practical questions

Before we begin.

Can you improve my current website?

Yes. We first review the platform, account access, content and the changes you need. We then recommend an update, redesign or migration based on what is practical.

Do I need an app as well as a website?

Not always. We look at how often people will use it and what they need to do. An existing tool or a mobile-friendly website may be enough; a custom app makes sense when it solves a clear, repeat-use problem.

How much will my project cost?

The scope, number of screens or pages, integrations and content requirements determine the quote. Tell us your priorities and budget, and we will explain the options and costs before you decide.

Who owns the website and accounts?

Your domain and service accounts should be registered in your name. Ownership of project-specific design and code, and any third-party licences, is set out in the agreement, along with the handover arrangements.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. Updates, hosting support and maintenance can be included in an agreed support arrangement or quoted as separate work. We explain the coverage and response expectations before you choose.

Can we work together from another city or country?

Yes. We can work remotely in English and Chinese. For overseas projects, we confirm the working hours, currency, payment stages and delivery arrangements in the proposal.
