From finding creators to reviewing content.

    We consider your business, audience, preferred content style and budget when identifying Melbourne Xiaohongshu creators and discussing potential collaborations.

    • Present available options for your consideration

Practical questions

Before we begin.

Can we book only part of the service?

Yes. Creator sourcing, scheduling, script review and video review can be combined to suit the project. Responsibilities and deliverables are confirmed in the proposal.

Do you film and edit the videos?

This service focuses on liaison, planning, coordination and review. Filming and editing are handled by the agreed creator or production provider. Any additional production support is scoped and quoted separately.

Do you guarantee views or sales?

We do not promise a fixed number of views, followers or sales. We deliver the agreed planning and coordination work and gather available data to inform future decisions.

Can this be combined with a website project?

Yes. A campaign can work alongside a website, campaign page or booking and enquiry flow, subject to platform rules and business needs. Promotion can also be commissioned separately.
