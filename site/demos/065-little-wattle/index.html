Three rooms, one big backyard

Each room has its own space and routine, and everyone meets outside under the wattle tree.

Group sizes follow Queensland's educator-to-child ratios.

Seedlings

6 weeks – 2 years

Up to 12 babies with 3 educators. A calm sleep room with cots, a sunny floor for rolling and crawling, and your baby's routine from home followed as closely as we can.

  • Cot room
  • Bottles from home
  • Daily diary

Sprouts

2 – 3 years

Up to 15 toddlers with 3 educators. Big-body play, the mud kitchen, first friendships and toilet learning at their own pace.

  • Mud kitchen
  • Toilet learning
  • Rest beds

Gumnuts

3 – 5 years · kindy

Up to 22 children with 2 educators, one an early childhood teacher. A play-based kindy program for the year before Prep, with plenty of outdoor time.

    A note for families

    Fees & the Child Care Subsidy

    RoomAgesPer day
    Seedlings6 wks – 2 yrs$142

    Sample prices for a full day (6:30am–6:00pm). Meals, nappies, sunscreen, bedding and incursions are included.

    What's the Child Care Subsidy? The Australian Government's Child Care Subsidy (CCS) can help with the cost of approved care. It's paid to the centre and taken off your fees, so you pay the gap. How much you get depends on things like your family's income and circumstances.

    General information only. Your subsidy and gap fee depend on your own situation — we're happy to walk you through an example on your tour.
