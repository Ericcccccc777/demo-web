      Getting there

      Leave the car at home

      We're on the riverbank lawns at South Bank. Parking nearby fills fast on a Friday night, so public transport is the easy option.

      Train

      South Brisbane station, then about six minutes' walk towards the river.

      Bus

      Busway stops are a short walk away — look for services to South Bank.

      River ferry

      Ferries stop at the South Bank terminals, then follow the lanterns along the path.

      Bike or walk

      Bike racks at the North Gate; the riverside path runs right past the market.

      Access

      Step-free paths throughout, accessible toilets by the Info Tent, and a quiet hour on the Fig Tree Lawn from 5–6 pm.

      Rain or shine

      We run in light rain. If storms are forecast, we'll post a decision by 2 pm on the Friday.

      Become a stallholder

      Got something good? Bring it to the river.

      We keep a mix of street food, makers and music, and we save a few spots each month for first-time stallholders.

      • 3 × 3 m sites from $65 a Friday, power +$15 (sample prices).
      • Bump-in from 3 pm, lights on at 5 pm, packed down by 10.45 pm.
      • Food stalls need a current council food licence and a fire extinguisher.
      • Makers: handmade or designed by you — no resellers, please.
      • Buskers apply free for 20-minute sets at Busker's Corner.
