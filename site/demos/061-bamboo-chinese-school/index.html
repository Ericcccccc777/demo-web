shēng声

Four tones, one little ma四个声调，一个 ma

In Mandarin, the pitch of your voice changes the word. Say ma four ways and you get four different words. Tap a card to hear its tone — the red dot rides the pitch line.普通话里，音高一变，意思就变。同一个 ma 念成四个声调，就是四个不同的字。点一下卡片听听声调，小红点会沿着音高线走。

Sound plays only when you tap. Keyboard: 1 2 3 4. The small numbers show pitch on a 1–5 scale, 5 being highest.只有点击时才会发声，也可以按键盘 1 2 3 4。卡片上的小数字表示音高，1最低，5最高。

Your turn: which tone is it?考考你：这是第几声？

Press play, listen, then pick the matching pitch line. Five rounds — one red star for every right answer.按下播放，仔细听，再选出对应的音高线。一共五题，答对一题得一颗小红星。

5 rounds共5题

Which line matches what you heard?你听到的是哪一条音高线？

Press play to start — the lines wake up once there’s a tone to match.先按播放开始，有了题目，下面的音高线就可以选啦。

Class rhyme声调口诀一声平，二声扬，三声拐弯，四声降。One stays flat, two goes up, three bends round, four drops down.一句话记住四个声调的走向。

Tongue twister绕口令妈妈骑马，马慢，妈妈骂马。Māma qí mǎ, mǎ màn, māma mà mǎ.Mum rides the horse; the horse is slow; Mum scolds the horse.一句话里藏着好几个 ma，试试看能不能说清楚！

    Good questions常见问题

    Does anyone at home need to speak Chinese?家里需要有人会说中文吗？

    No. Plenty of our students start from zero, and worksheets come home with pinyin so parents can follow along.不需要。很多学生都是零基础，带回家的练习纸都标有拼音，家长也能跟着看。

    Simplified or traditional characters?学简体字还是繁体字？

    We teach simplified characters with pinyin. Families who read traditional are very welcome — we point out both forms when it helps.我们教简体字和拼音。习惯繁体字的家庭也非常欢迎，需要时我们会把两种写法都讲一讲。

    What if we miss a weekend?缺了一次课怎么办？

    Each week’s worksheet and song goes home in a folder so you can catch up. Missed lessons aren’t refunded.每周的练习纸和儿歌都会放进文件夹带回家，方便补上。缺课不退费。
