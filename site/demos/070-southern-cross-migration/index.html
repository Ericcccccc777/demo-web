Questions

Before you book.

Can you guarantee my visa will be granted?

No. Visa decisions are made by the government, and no agent can promise an outcome. We can explain your options honestly and help you prepare a complete, well-evidenced application.

Are your agents registered?

Advice is given by registered migration agents. You can check any agent's registration on the official public register before you book with anyone.

Is the pathway finder advice?

No — it's general information to help you prepare. It doesn't assess eligibility, and requirements change. Your agent will confirm what applies to you.

Can I book for my family members too?

Yes. Tell us who is included when you book so we can allow enough time.
