03A slow bar

A slow bar is a counter where the coffee sets the pace.

Take the stool at the end of the bench and we'll brew your filter by hand — weighed, bloomed and poured in slow circles. You're welcome to watch, ask questions, or simply sit with the window.

  1. 0:00
    Weigh15 g of coffee, ground medium-fine just before brewing.
  2. 0:15
    Bloom45 g of water at 94°C. The bed rises, breathes and settles.
  3. 0:45
    PourSlow spirals from the centre out, to 250 g in total.
  4. 3:30
    RestIt drains through, and we pour it into a warmed cup.
