Two and a half hours, two candles, one very good smell.

What goes in

Four materials, chosen on purpose.

These are the choices we've made for our own candles — what we pour with, and why.

Soy wax

A soft container wax we pour cool and slow, so the top sets smooth and the melt pool stays even. Spills lift off with warm soapy water.

Cotton wicks

Braided cotton, sized jar by jar and burn-tested until the pool reaches the glass in about two hours.

Fragrance

Fragrance and essential-oil blends mixed in-house in small batches, then cured in the wax for two weeks before they're sold.

Glass & tins

Heavy smoked-glass tumblers made to outlive the candle. Bring five empties back to the pour room and we'll clean and reuse them.

  1. First burn: 2–3 hours, until the melt pool reaches the edge.
  2. Trim the wick to about 5 mm before every light.
  3. Four hours at most, then let it cool completely.
  4. Away from draughts, pets, children and curtains.
  5. Never leave a burning candle unattended.
