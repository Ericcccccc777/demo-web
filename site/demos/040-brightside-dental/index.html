    Calm-visit tips

    1. Tell us when you book — we’ll allow extra time and never rush you.
    2. Agree on a stop signal. Raise your hand and we pause, every time.
    3. Bring headphones for music. Feeling cold? We have blankets.
    4. Book a morning visit, so there’s less time to worry.
    5. Ask us to explain each step first — or not, if you’d rather not know.

    General information only — not a diagnosis. Your dentist will advise on your own teeth.
