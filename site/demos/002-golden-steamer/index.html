Allergies & dietary needs

Most dim sum contains wheat, soy and sesame; har gow, siu mai and prawn cheung fun contain shellfish. Tell us about allergies when you sit down and we’ll steer you through the trolley — but we can’t promise a nut- or shellfish-free kitchen.

    Tea & trolley hours

    Trolleys roll at the weekend.

    On Saturdays and Sundays the carts do laps from opening until 14:30 — flag one down, point, and we stamp your card. On weekdays, everything is cooked to order from the menu.

    Weekend queue tips

    The line moves fast if you know the drill.

    1. 一

      Come before 10:15. The first sitting fills the room within twenty minutes.

    2. 二

      Give your group size at the door. We call names in English, Cantonese and Mandarin.

    3. 三

      Tables for two move fastest. Groups of eight or more can book a round table on weekdays.

    4. 四

      Bring the kids. Prams park by the window and high chairs are always ready.
