Club news

From the noticeboard

  • Mon 21 Sep

    Pitch 2 lights are on

    The new lights on Pitch 2 are finished, so Tuesday juniors can train until 7:15 pm all winter. Thanks to every family who sold a raffle ticket.

  • Fri 25 Sep

    Working bee · Sun 11 Oct

    Goal nets, a canteen clean-out and fresh paint for the scoreboard. 9 am start, sausage sizzle at noon. Bring gloves.

  • Wed 16 Sep

    Teen referee course

    Players aged 15 and up can learn to referee junior games over two Saturday mornings in November. Spots are limited to twelve.
