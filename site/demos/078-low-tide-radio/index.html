    Liner notes

    Songs for the long way home

    “We write for the tram window, the bike path and the walk along the creek when you're not ready for the night to end.”

    The band started as a Tuesday-night jam in a Brunswick share house: two guitars, a borrowed electric piano and a drum kit held together with gaffer tape. The name came from a transistor radio someone left on a pier at low tide, still playing to nobody.

    Saltwater Hours was tracked live to tape over one long Melbourne winter in a converted milk bar in Brunswick East — fridge unplugged, tram bells left in. Most songs are first or second takes.

    Live, the songs stretch out: longer intros, a slow cover or two of songs our parents played in the car, and an encore if the room asks nicely.
