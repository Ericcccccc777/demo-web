    Pool type

    Do I need to be home?

    No. Leave the gate unlocked (or give us the code) and keep the dog inside. You'll get the photo report either way.

    What happens if it storms?

    If lightning's about we'll pause and come back the same day or first thing the next. Big storms often mean a free top-up visit to clear debris.

    Salt, chlorine or mineral?

    We service all three. Salt pools still need testing — the chlorinator makes chlorine, it doesn't balance the pH for you.
