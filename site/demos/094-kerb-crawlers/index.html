      Weekend mornings

      Lessons

      Groups of six at a council skate park in Brunswick — we text the exact spot the day before. Boards, helmets and pads are included.

      First push · ages 7–12

      Sat 9–10am · $35 per session (sample). Standing, pushing, stopping and falling safely.

      Basics · teens & adults

      Sat 10.30–11.30am · $40 (sample). Carving, tic-tacs and your first kick-turn.

      Bowl basics · ages 10+

      Sun 9–10.30am · $50 (sample). Pumping, rock to fakie, and dropping in when you’re ready.

      Private coaching

      By arrangement · $70 an hour (sample). One rider, your goals.

      Pick a session

      Helmets are compulsory in every lesson. Riders under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign on the day. General information only — skateboarding carries a risk of injury.
