Moving up

Collect the stickers

Every lane has a sticker. When a teacher sees the skill at the end of term, it goes on the swim card and your child moves up a lane.

  1. Tadpole

    Gets in and out safely with a grown-up.

  2. Turtle

    Blows bubbles and floats with a little help.

  3. Dolphin

    Face in, glides and kicks 5 metres.

  4. Shark

    Swims 25 metres and treads water for a minute.

  5. Squad

    Four strokes and 200 metres without stopping.

Out of lessons

Water safety at home

  • Watch, don't browse

    Keep young children within arm's reach and your phone in your pocket.

  • Close the gate

    Check the pool fence and self-closing gate every time you leave the yard.

  • Learn CPR

    A short course gives you the skills to help in the minutes that matter.

  • Swim between the flags

    At the beach, swim between the red and yellow flags with a grown-up.

General information only — lessons build skills and confidence but never replace active adult supervision.

From the pool deck

First-lesson questions

Do I need to get in the water?

For Tadpoles, yes — one parent or carer swims with each child. From Turtles up, you watch from the bench by the window.

What if my child cries?

Plenty do in week one. Teachers go gently, and nobody is ever dunked or made to put their face in before they're ready.

Do babies need swim nappies?

Yes — a reusable swim nappy over a disposable one for anyone under three, please.

How do they move up a lane?

Teachers check skills in the last fortnight of term. Earn the sticker and we'll suggest the next lane for the following term.
