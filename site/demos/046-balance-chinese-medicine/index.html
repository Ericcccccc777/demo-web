贰

针灸Acupuncture

Very fine, single-use needles are placed at chosen points and left for 20–30 minutes while you rest. Most people feel a dull ache, warmth or heaviness around the needle — not the sharpness of an injection.医师在选定的穴位施以极细的一次性针具，留针二十至三十分钟，期间您可静卧休息。多数人针下会有酸、胀、温热或沉重感，与打针的刺痛感不同。

Please tell us if you’re pregnant, take blood thinners, or tend to feel faint around needles.如您怀孕、正在服用抗凝药物或有晕针史，请提前告知医师。

中药调理Herbal consultation

We ask about sleep, digestion, energy and anything else you’d like to raise, look at your tongue and feel your pulse. If herbs are suggested, they come as easy-to-mix granules or as traditional raw herbs.医师会询问您的睡眠、消化、精力等情况，并察看舌象、切脉。如建议服用中药，可选择冲服方便的颗粒剂或传统饮片。

Bring a list of any medicines and supplements you take — some herbs don’t mix with them.请携带您正在服用的药物及保健品清单——部分中药不宜与其同服。

拔罐Cupping

Glass or silicone cups create gentle suction over the back and shoulders, usually for five to fifteen minutes. They can leave round marks that fade over several days — worth knowing before a beach weekend.以玻璃罐或硅胶罐在背部、肩部形成轻柔吸力，通常留罐五至十五分钟。拔罐后可能留下圆形印记，数日内逐渐消退——如周末打算去海边，不妨提前考虑。

五行The Five Elements

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water are a classical Chinese way of describing change and how things relate. Tap an element to see its traditional associations, then switch between the two cycles.木、火、土、金、水——五行——是中国古典思想中描述万物变化与相互关系的框架。点击任一元素，查看其传统对应；并可切换相生、相克两种循环。

Shared as cultural background from classical Chinese thought — not a diagnosis or a health claim.以上为中国传统文化背景介绍，并非诊断或疗效说明。

初诊须知What to expect

  1. Before you come就诊前Wear loose clothing and have a light meal an hour or two before.请穿着宽松衣物，并在一两个小时前少量进食。

  2. Consultation · about 20 minutes问诊 · 约二十分钟Your health history and questions, then we look at your tongue and take your pulse.了解您的健康状况并问诊，随后察舌、切脉。

  3. Treatment治疗Acupuncture, cupping or both, while you rest in a warm, quiet room.在温暖安静的房间中静卧，进行针灸、拔罐或两者结合。

  4. Afterwards治疗后Drink some water, take it easy, and we’ll talk through a plan — no long packages.多喝水，适当休息；我们会与您商量后续安排，不捆绑长期疗程。
