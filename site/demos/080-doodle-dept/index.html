Join the dots

How a commission usually goes, from first hello to files in your inbox. Small jobs skip a dot or two.

  1. 1

    Say hello

    A 20-minute call or email: what it’s for, where it will live, budget and deadline.

  2. 2

    Roughs

    Two or three pencil thumbnails within a week. Pick one, mix two, or send me back to the desk.

  3. 3

    Line art

    Clean black lines, like this page. One round of tweaks before any colour goes down.

  4. 4

    Colour

    Your palette or mine. Flat, bright and checked in print as well as on screen.

  5. 5

    Hand-off

    Final files, the licence letter and a colouring-page version for the kids in your life.

How far ahead should I book?

Picture books: four to six months. Packaging and spot drawings: usually two to four weeks. Ask anyway if it’s urgent.

Who owns the artwork?

You get a licence for the agreed use — for example, packaging sold in Australia for three years. Copyright stays with the studio unless we agree on a buy-out.

Do you paint murals outside Melbourne?

Yes, around Victoria and interstate. Travel and accommodation are quoted separately.
