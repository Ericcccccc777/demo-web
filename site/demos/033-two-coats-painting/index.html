    How a job runs

    1. 1

      Walk-through

      We visit, measure, look at the prep and give you a written, itemised quote.

    2. 2

      Colours

      Use the visualiser, book a consult, or tell us the codes you already love.

    3. 3

      Prep

      Wash, scrape, fill, sand and prime. Most of the finish is decided here.

    4. 4

      Two coats

      Cut in by brush, rolled twice, with drying time between. No shortcuts.

    5. 5

      Walk-through, again

      We check every wall with you, touch up anything missed and leave it tidy.
