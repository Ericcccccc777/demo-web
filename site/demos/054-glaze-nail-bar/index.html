    Aftercare

    Keep the shine

    Six habits that decide whether a set lasts ten days or three weeks.

    1. 1

      Oil, twice a day

      A drop of cuticle oil morning and night keeps gel flexible and cuticles soft.

    2. 2

      Gloves for chores

      Detergents dull gloss faster than anything. Gloves for dishes and cleaning.

    3. 3

      Don’t pick

      Peeling gel takes layers of your own nail with it. Book a removal instead.

    4. 4

      Easy on day one

      Skip very hot baths, saunas and long swims for the first 24 hours.

    5. 5

      Nails aren’t tools

      Use a coin, not a nail, for can tabs, lids and stickers.

    6. 6

      Book the infill

      Builder gel and extensions want an infill every three to four weeks.

    Allergic to gel or acrylates, or had a reaction before? Tell us before your visit and we’ll suggest a regular polish instead. General information only.
