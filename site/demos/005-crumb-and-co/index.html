Our slow ferment

36 hours from flour to crust. The recipe card pinned above the bench:

  1. Day 1 · 4 pmFeed Mabel, our starter — rye flour, water, a warm corner.
  2. 9 pmMix, rest, then four rounds of stretch-and-fold, half an hour apart.
  3. OvernightShaped loaves sleep in the cool room at 4°C. That's where the flavour comes from.
  4. Day 2 · 4:30 amEach loaf is scored by hand — the “ear” is the baker's signature.
  5. 6:30 amBaked hot with steam for about 40 minutes, then onto the racks.

Why so slow? Flavour, a crackly crust, and a loaf that keeps for days.
