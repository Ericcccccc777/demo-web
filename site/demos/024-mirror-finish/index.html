03 / Process

Same five steps. Every car.

  1. Inspect

    A walk-around under LED light, a paint-depth gauge on each panel, and photos of anything we find.

  2. Decontaminate

    Snow foam, a two-bucket wash, iron remover and a clay bar until the paint feels like glass.

  3. Correct

    Machine polishing in stages, checked with a swirl-finder torch between every pass.

  4. Protect

    Sealant or ceramic applied panel by panel, then cured — under IR lamps if it's cold.

  5. Hand over

    We walk the car with you in daylight and leave a care card for the first few washes.

Do you need power or water?

No. The van carries its own water tank, a quiet generator and a pop-up shade. We just need room to walk around the car.

Can you work in an apartment car park?

Usually, yes — we switch to a rinseless wash underground. Check with your building manager first; we can send a note they can approve.

What if it rains?

Washes and interiors go ahead under our shade. Correction and ceramic need dry, stable weather, so we'll move you to the next free day.

How do I look after a ceramic coating?

Wash every two weeks with a pH-neutral shampoo, skip brush car washes, and book a maintenance wash every few months.
