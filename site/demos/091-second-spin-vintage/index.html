    Four decades, one rack

    Page 08Sizing guide

    Vintage sizes run small

    A 1970s size 14 is closer to a modern AU 10, so we ignore old labels. Every piece is measured flat, in centimetres, and tagged with the modern size it actually fits.

    Our size tags compared with modern Australian sizes
    Our tagFits likeA · pit to pitWaist, flat
    XSAU 6–844–47 cm32–35 cm
    SAU 8–1047–50 cm35–38 cm
    MAU 1250–53 cm38–41 cm
    LAU 1453–57 cm41–45 cm
    XLAU 16–1857–62 cm45–50 cm

    Lay your favourite jumper flat and measure armpit to armpit. Compare that number with the catalogue entry, not the old label — it's the only size that doesn't lie.
