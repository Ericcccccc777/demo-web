Our process

Four steps, no panic

The same calm rhythm for every couple, whichever package you choose.

  1. one

    A long lunch

    An unhurried first conversation over lunch or video: your guest list, your non-negotiables and the feeling you want. No charge, no obligation.

  2. two

    The plan

    Within ten days you'll have a written plan — budget, timeline, venue shortlist and the suppliers we'd trust with your day.

  3. three

    The details

    Tastings, fittings, styling sessions and a run sheet everyone signs off. We chase the confirmations so you don't have to.

  4. four

    The day

    We arrive before the florist and leave after the last guest's shuttle. You get to be present — that's the whole point.

Good questions

Before you ask

How far ahead should we book you?

For Saturdays in autumn and spring, twelve to eighteen months is comfortable. Fridays, Sundays and winter dates often have room at shorter notice — just ask.

Do you only work in the Hunter Valley?

Most of our weddings are in Pokolbin, Lovedale, Broke and Wollombi, but we travel to the Central Coast, Mudgee and Sydney for a travel fee.

Can we bring our own suppliers?

Of course. We'll happily work alongside people you already love and fill any gaps from our own list.

What happens if it rains?

Every plan has a wet-weather version, signed off before wedding week. Most estates we suggest have a covered ceremony option, and we bring the umbrellas.
