    How adoption works

    1. Find a match

      Use the filters, read the profiles and pick a few favourites.

    2. Tell us about home

      A relaxed chat about your household, yard and routine — no trick questions.

    3. Meet & greet

      Meet at the foster home. Bring the family, and your dog if you have one.

    4. Trial sleepover

      A week at your place with the foster carer a text away, then it's official.

    Adoption fees

    • Dogs $300–$420
    • Cats & kittens $150–$220
    • Rabbits $90
    • Guinea pig pairs $80

    Sample prices. Every animal goes home desexed, vaccinated, microchipped, wormed and flea-treated.

    Can I adopt if I rent?

    Yes — check that your lease allows pets, and we'll help you put together a pet application for your landlord.

    Do you adopt outside Newcastle?

    We place animals across the Hunter, from Lake Macquarie to Port Stephens and Maitland. Meet & greets happen at the foster home.

    What if it doesn't work out?

    Call us. Every animal can come back to us at any time, for any reason, and we'll find them the right home.

    Foster care

    Lend us your couch

    We don't have kennels. Every animal lives in a volunteer's home until adoption day — that's where shy cats learn to trust, and big dogs learn house manners.

    • You bring the companyA spare room or a quiet corner, a routine, and a lot of patience.
    • We cover the restFood, bedding, vet bills and a foster coordinator who answers texts.
    • Kitten season is comingOctober to March is our busiest time — bottle-babies need night feeds.

      Volunteer

      Lend a hand

      No experience needed — just reliability and a soft spot for animals. Pick a job that fits your week.

      • Dog walkers

        Saturday and Sunday mornings, an hour or two. Leads and poo bags supplied.

      • Cat cuddlers

        Help shy cats learn that people are OK. Weekday arvos at foster homes.

      • Transport crew

        Drive animals to vet visits and meet & greets. Your own car, fuel reimbursed.

      • Laundry legends

        Wash and fold the towels and blankets that never stop coming.

      • Event helpers

        Adoption days, sausage sizzles and market stalls around town.

      • Photos & words

        Take the portraits and write the profiles that get animals noticed.
