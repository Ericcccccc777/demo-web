    How we work

    4
    step 01

    We start with what the day-to-day really looks like, who you’d work with, and what good looks like after three months.

    step 02

    A phone screen, a skills check where it matters, and references before interviews — not after.

    step 03

    We set up interviews and pass on honest feedback within a day, whichever side of the table you’re on.

    step 04

    We call both sides after a month and again after three, so small niggles don’t turn into resignations.
