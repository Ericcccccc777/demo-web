    Before you sit down

    House rules

    Seven small courtesies that keep the room the way you found it.

    1. I

      Knock three times

      You found the door. That's the hard part done.

    2. II

      Voices low, glasses high

      It's a small room — keep conversation at table level so everyone can hear the band.

    3. III

      Calls in the laneway

      Photos are welcome. Phone calls happen outside, under the lamp.

    4. IV

      Walk-ins up to six

      Larger groups book ahead so we can set the Emerald Room.

    5. V

      Dress how you like

      Suits, denim, sequins — all fine. Just leave the thongs for the beach.

    6. VI

      Water on every table

      We serve responsibly and check ID for anyone who looks under 25.

    7. VII

      Be kind to the band

      Requests are welcome on a coaster. Heckles are not.
