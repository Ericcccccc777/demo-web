    Can I skip a box?

    Any time before the 20th of the month — skip, pause or swap items from your account.

    What if my pet hates something?

    Tell us and we'll swap it in the next box. No questions — just treats.

    Do you deliver outside Sydney?

    Not yet. We deliver across Sydney, and the Inner West is free.

    What about allergies?

    Pick them in the builder and we'll leave those ingredients out. Chat to your vet before changing your pet's diet.
