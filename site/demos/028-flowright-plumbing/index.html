    03 / Fix the leak

    Go on — tighten it

    A weeping compression nut is one of the most common call-outs we get. Drag the spanner sideways to turn the nut, or use the buttons or arrow keys.

    Drag the spanner →
    Tightness0%

    Loose — dripping about four times a second.

    Playful estimate: one drip ≈ 0.05 mL. A real weeping joint usually needs a new olive or fitting — overtightening can crack it — so call us rather than reaching for the shifter.
