    Good to know

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Yes — tables of 2 to 6 are walk-in only. Fridays after 7pm there's usually a short wait; we'll text you when a grill frees up.

    Vegetarian or gluten-free?

    King oyster mushrooms, tofu, corn cheese and most banchan are vegetarian. Tell us about coeliac or allergies when you arrive and the kitchen will walk you through the marinades.

    Kids?

    Very welcome until 9pm. We'll put them at the end of the table, well away from the coals.

    Will I smell like barbecue?

    A little. Every grill has its own extraction hood, and there are jacket bags on the hooks by the door.
