Grounding · step 1 of 5

Five, four, three, two, one. Take each step at your own pace.

  1. 5

    Five things you can see

    Let your eyes move slowly around you. A corner of the window, a colour, the texture of a wall.

  2. 4

    Four things you can feel

    Your feet on the floor, the weight of your hands, the air on your skin, the chair behind your back.

  3. 3

    Three things you can hear

    Near sounds and far ones — traffic, a fridge humming, your own breathing.

  4. 2

    Two things you can smell

    If nothing comes, remember a smell you like: rain on the footpath, toast, the sea.

  5. 1

    One thing you can taste

    Or take one slow sip of water. Then let your breath settle for a moment.

That's all five. Notice how you're sitting now — nothing needs to be different. You can come back to this whenever you like.

How I can help

You don't need to have the right words yet.

Most people arrive with something that's been building for a while. We can start wherever you are. I often work with:

  • Anxiety and worry — the racing mind at 3am, or a low hum that never quite switches off.
  • Grief and loss — of people, pets, health, work, or the life you'd planned.
  • Stress and burnout — especially for carers, health workers and teachers.
  • Big changes — moving to Tasmania, separating, retiring, becoming a parent.
  • Relationships — with partners, family, or yourself.
  • Low mood — when things have lost their colour and you're not sure why.

I work with adults, one to one. I'm not a crisis service — if you need help urgently, please call Lifeline on 13 11 14 or 000.

What sessions are like

Fifty minutes that are entirely yours.

We talk, and you set the pace. Some weeks we'll look closely at one thing; other weeks we'll sit with how things are. I might suggest small practices to try between sessions, but there's no homework you can fail.

Sessions are in a quiet upstairs room with a window onto a garden, or by video or phone if that's easier. Most people come weekly or fortnightly to begin with.

In the room

  • Tea, water and tissues within reach — no need to ask
  • A few quiet minutes to arrive before we start talking
  • Time at the end to wind down before you head back out

What you say stays between us, within the limits of the law — I'll explain exactly what that means in our first session.

Your first session

A chance to see if we're a good fit.

  1. Before

    You'll get a short email with directions, parking and a one-page form. Fill in only what you're comfortable with.

  2. During

    We'll talk about what brings you here and what you'd like to be different. You can ask me anything about how I work. You don't have to tell me everything.

  3. After

    We decide together whether to keep going. There's no obligation, and if someone else would suit you better I'll help you find them.

Questions

Things people often wonder.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can contact me directly. If you do have a referral from your GP, bring it along and we can talk about whether any rebate might apply.

What if I cry, or don't know what to say?

Both are completely okay. There are tissues, water and silence in the room, and none of them need explaining.

Can we meet online?

Yes — by video or phone, anywhere in Tasmania. It helps to find a private spot where you won't be overheard.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies a lot. Some people come for a handful of sessions; others prefer longer. We'll check in regularly about how it's going.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give 48 hours' notice if you can. Life happens — if it's short notice, just let me know and we'll work it out.

Get in touch

Send a short note, whenever you're ready.

You don't need to explain much. A name and the best way to reach you is enough, and I usually reply within two working days.

Serves 4 · 40 minutes

  1. Soften a chopped onion in a little oil for five minutes.
  2. Add 1 kg of peeled, cubed pumpkin and a litre of stock.
  3. Simmer for 25 minutes, until the pumpkin is soft.
  4. Blend until smooth and season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
