    Questions at the counter

    Is this plant safe for my cat or dog?

    Each plate shows a paw symbol when the plant is commonly listed as non-toxic to cats and dogs. Lists vary and animals react differently, so treat it as general information and check with your vet — especially for chewers.

    My flat gets almost no sun. Anything?

    Set the key to “Low” light. Zanzibar gem, parlour palm, peace lily and bird’s nest fern all cope with a dim room, as long as you water less.

    Do you sell pots and potting mix?

    Yes — glazed and terracotta pots from 100 mm to 400 mm, plus our own indoor, native and herb mixes by the bag or the scoop.

    Can I bring in a plant that’s struggling?

    Please do. Saturday-morning plant clinic is free; if it needs repotting we’ll quote first.
