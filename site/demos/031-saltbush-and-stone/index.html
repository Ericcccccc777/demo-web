      What we do

      From a sketch on site to the first year of growth.

      Small team, local plants, no lawn required. We work across Fremantle and the southern suburbs, on everything from courtyards to quarter-acre blocks.

      Native garden design

      A plan drawn for your block’s sun, soil and wind, with a plant palette chosen so each season has something in flower.

      • Site walk & soil test
      • Concept & planting plans
      • Local species schedule

      From $1,450for a full planting plan · garden walk $180, credited

      Water-wise makeovers

      Lawn out, natives in. We rework thirsty front yards into low-water gardens with drip irrigation on a timer.

      • Lawn removal
      • Soil improvement
      • Drip irrigation
      • Mulch & planting

      From $9,500for a typical front yard

      Paving & stone

      Limestone paths, crazy paving, seating walls and dry-stone edging — laid so winter rain soaks into the garden, not the drain.

      • Limestone & granite
      • Permeable paths
      • Low retaining walls

      From $140/m²for limestone paving, laid

      Seasonal care

      Four visits a year, timed to the seasons: pruning, feeding, mulching and an irrigation check before summer.

      • Seasonal pruning
      • Irrigation audit
      • Weeding & mulch

      From $320per seasonal visit
