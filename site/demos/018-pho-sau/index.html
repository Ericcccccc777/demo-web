Blanch the bones

Beef marrow and knuckle bones go into a hard boil for ten minutes, then out into the sink for a scrub. It looks wasteful. It’s the reason the broth ends up clear, not grey.

Cách ăn phởHow to eat phở · the herb plate

  1. 1Húng quế · Thai basil. Tear the leaves in over the bowl.
  2. 2Ngò gai · Sawtooth coriander. Louder than coriander; start with one leaf.
  3. 3Giá · Bean sprouts. Raw for crunch, or push them under to soften.
  4. 4Chanh · Lime. One wedge, taste, then decide on the second.
  5. 5Ớt · Bird’s-eye chilli. Properly hot; two slices is a fine start.
07

The cook’s five rulesPinned above the pass

  1. 01

    Broth first

    Before herbs, before sauce: one spoonful of the broth on its own. Twelve hours went into it.

  2. 02

    Tear, don’t pile

    Add herbs a few at a time so they stay bright instead of stewing on top.

  3. 03

    Sauce on the side

    Hoisin and chilli sauce go in the little dish, for dipping the beef — not into the bowl.

  4. 04

    Chopsticks and spoon

    One in each hand. Lift noodles onto the spoon, add a little broth, then eat.

  5. 05

    Slurp

    Encouraged, honestly. Hot noodles need the air.

6

Why “Sáu”?

Sáu is Vietnamese for six. Six stockpots live on our back stove, lit an hour apart, so one is always reaching hour twelve. This issue follows pot Nº 6.
