5Stop 5 · FAQ第5站 · 常见问题

Questions from the passenger seat.学员最常问的问题

How many logbook hours do I need?我需要在日志里记多少小时？

In NSW, learners under 25 generally need 120 hours of supervised driving in their logbook, including 20 hours at night. An hour-long lesson with a licensed instructor can count as three logbook hours, for up to ten lessons. Rules change, so check the current NSW requirements before relying on this.在新州，25岁以下的学员一般需要在日志中记录120小时的陪驾练习，其中包括20小时夜间驾驶。与持牌教练上一小时课，通常可按三小时记入日志，最多计十节课。规定可能调整，请以新州政府的最新要求为准。

I've never driven before. Where do we start?我从来没开过车，要从哪里开始？

In a quiet car park. We cover the controls, mirrors and smooth stops before you ever see a main road.从安静的停车场开始。我们先熟悉车辆操作、后视镜和平稳刹车，然后才上大路。

Can you pick me up from school or work?可以到学校或公司接我吗？

Yes — anywhere in our pickup area around Burwood, Strathfield, Concord, Croydon, Ashfield and Five Dock.可以。Burwood、Strathfield、Concord、Croydon、Ashfield和Five Dock一带都能接送。

What car will I learn in?用什么车练习？

A small automatic hatchback with dual controls, so your instructor can brake if needed. L plates are on us.一辆小型自动挡掀背车，配有双控制系统，必要时教练可以帮你刹车。L牌由我们提供。
