      05/07E-bikes

      Test rides Thursdays, 4–7 pm

      Red Hill and Mount Ainslie, minus the dread.

      We sell, service and set up e-bikes for commuters and cargo families. Bring your helmet for a test ride — we'll set the saddle height and walk you through the assist levels before you roll out.

      Mid-drive motor

      Sits at the cranks and works through your gears. Natural on steep climbs; wears chains faster, so budget for one a year.

      Hub motor

      Sits in the rear wheel. Simpler, quieter and cheaper to run — best for flatter commutes and bike paths.

      • 01Frosty night? Bring the battery inside and charge it at room temperature.
      • 02Parking it for a while? Leave the battery at 30–60% and top it up monthly.
      • 03Wash with a damp cloth — never a pressure hose near the motor or connectors.
      06/07Commuter notes

      General notes · read before riding

      Riding to work in Canberra

      Six notes from the workshop bench — for frosty mornings, swooping season and everything in between.

      1. Frost on the paths

        Winter mornings often start below zero. Drop tyre pressure a little for grip and take bike-path corners wide and upright.

      2. Swooping season

        Magpies swoop roughly August to November. Walk past known nests or take the next street; don't stop and stare the bird down.

      3. Lights at both ends

        White up front and red at the back from dusk. A flashing rear light helps on grey afternoons too.

      4. Wind off the lake

        A calm morning around the lake can be a headwind by five. Pack a spare layer and plan for a slower ride home.

      5. Lock it like you mean it

        A D-lock through the frame and rear wheel, to something bolted down. Quick-release wheels can go walking — ask us about locking skewers.

      6. Ring early, pass wide

        Shared paths are busy with walkers, prams and dogs. A bell two seconds before you pass beats a surprised shout.
