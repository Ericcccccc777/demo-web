    How long do natives last?

    Most give you one to two weeks in a clean vase with fresh water every couple of days — and banksia, gum nuts and wattle dry beautifully afterwards.

    Can I have natives only?

    Of course. Garden roses and lisianthus are the only non-natives we stock — leave them out and it’s natives all the way.

    Do you deliver on Sundays?

    Not at the moment. We deliver Tuesday to Saturday; order by 11am for same-day delivery in Zone A.
