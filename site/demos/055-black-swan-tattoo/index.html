Process & deposits

  1. Enquire

    Send your idea, placement and rough size. Flash off the wall is first in, first served.

    Fifteen minutes in the studio or on a video call — we sketch, size it on you and quote.

  3. Deposit

    Eat beforehand, bring photo ID and wear clothes that give easy access to the spot.

Aftercare

  1. Hours 0–4

    Leave the wrap on for two to four hours, then wash gently with lukewarm water and fragrance-free soap.

  2. Days 1–14

    Pat dry with clean paper towel and apply a thin layer of unscented balm two or three times a day.

  3. Hands off

    No swimming, baths or sunbaking for two weeks. Let flakes fall on their own — don't pick.

  4. Check in

    Some redness and tenderness is normal for a few days. If it gets worse or you feel unwell, see a GP.

General information only, not medical advice. Your artist will give you written aftercare that suits your tattoo.
