Good to know

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Every course starts from centring, and our teachers have seen every wobble there is.

What should I wear?

Short nails, sleeves you can push up and clothes you don't mind getting clay on. We lend aprons.

How long until I get my pots?

About three weeks: drying, a bisque firing, glazing, then the glaze firing.

Do you run kids' classes?

School-holiday handbuilding for ages 8–14, with a grown-up nearby. Ask us for dates.
