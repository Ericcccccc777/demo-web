Sold out? Here’s why

Birria braises overnight, so we make one big pot a day. When it’s gone, it’s gone — we’d rather sell out than serve yesterday’s.

Good to know

Are dogs welcome?

Always. There’s a water bowl hanging off the back step.

What if it rains?

Light rain, the awning goes out and we keep cooking. Storm warnings, we pack up and mark the stop cancelled on the map.

Can I pre-order?

Not for single orders — the queue moves fast. For 10 or more, call ahead and we’ll prep extra.

Where do you park exactly?

Spots move a little with events and roadworks, so look for the giant lime on the roof — you can’t miss it.
