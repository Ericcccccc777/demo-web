I'm not a strong swimmer. Can I still come?

Yes. Snorkellers can use a vest or noodle and join a guided swim. For intro dives you need to be comfortable in the water — we'll check in with you before you gear up.

Do I need a medical?

Everyone who scuba dives completes a medical questionnaire first. Some answers — asthma, heart conditions, recent surgery — need a doctor's sign-off before the trip, so read it early.

What if I get seasick?

The ride out is about 90 minutes. Sit outside near the back, look at the horizon, and take motion-sickness tablets before we leave if you usually need them.

Can I fly after diving?

Common guidance is to wait at least 18 hours after multiple dives before flying. Your instructor will talk you through it for your plans.

General information only — not medical advice or dive training. Conditions, sightings and sites vary every day.
