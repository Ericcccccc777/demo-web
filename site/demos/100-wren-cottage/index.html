Chapter III

Out and about

A few general ideas for your days. Check conditions and opening times before you head off.

Ride the rail trail

Flat, easy riding through the valley. Bikes can be hired in town; we’ll pack you a snack.

Autumn colour

Around April and May the town’s street trees turn gold and red — the whole valley glows.

Up the mountain

Plateau walks, granite lookouts and, in winter, snow — all a scenic drive from the door.

River swims

The river runs right through town, with shallow spots for a dip on hot summer afternoons.

Cellar doors & breweries

The valley has plenty. We’re happy to suggest a local driver so nobody draws the short straw.

The snowfields

A day trip away in winter. Carry chains if the road signs say so and leave early.

Chapter IV

The small print

Sample house rules for this concept demo.

Arriving & leaving
Check-in 3–7pm (later by arrangement), check-out 10am. A lantern stays on for late arrivals.
Weekends
Friday and Saturday nights have a two-night minimum.
Children
Guests 12 and over — it’s a quiet cottage, and the Loft stairs are steep.
Pets
Assistance animals only, sorry. The wrens appreciate it.
Cancelling
Full refund up to 14 days before arrival; after that, 50% unless we can re-let the room.
Accessibility
The Wren Room is on the ground floor with one small step at the door. Ask us anything.
The fire
Firewood and kindling supplied from May to September.
Parking
Off-street for three cars at the side gate.
