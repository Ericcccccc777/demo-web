Spa etiquette

A few quiet agreements

  1. Arrive fifteen minutes earlyTime to change, settle in and fill out a short health form.
  2. Phones on silentThe treatment wing and pool room are screen-free, so everyone can switch off.
  3. Tell us what we should knowInjuries, pregnancy, allergies or recent surgery — it changes what we recommend.
  4. Robes and slippers are providedBring swimwear if you'd like to use the pool room.
  5. 24 hours to move or cancelLater changes may be charged, because your therapist's time is held for you.
  6. Guests 16 and overUnder-18s need a parent or guardian with them.
