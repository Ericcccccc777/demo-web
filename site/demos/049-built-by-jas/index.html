      Method

      How I coach

      Progressive overload

      Small, planned increases — a rep, a set or a few kilos — week by week. You’ll always know why today looks the way it does.

      Deloads on the calendar

      A lighter week is planned from day one, not taken when you’re already cooked. Recovery is part of the program.

      Technique before load

      Your first two weeks are about owning the movements. Lifts are filmed and reviewed with you, frame by frame.

      Weekly check-ins

      Five minutes every Sunday: sleep, stress, soreness and wins. The next week adjusts to your real life.

      FAQ

      Do I need to be fit to start?

      No. Week one is written around where you are now — the whole point of a 12-week block is that it starts sensibly and builds.

      What’s a deload week?

      A planned lighter week, with less volume and intensity, so your body can absorb the work you’ve done. It’s on the calendar from day one.

      Can I train with an injury or health condition?

      Talk to your GP or physio first. With their clearance I can adapt exercises and loads — but I don’t diagnose or treat injuries.

      Is the generated plan my actual program?

      No — it’s an illustration of how I structure a block. Your real program is written after the intro chat and a movement screen.

      What if I miss a week?

      Life happens. We shuffle the calendar rather than cramming sessions, and adjust at your Sunday check-in.
