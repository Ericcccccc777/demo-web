    Fair questions

    Do you do small jobs?

    Yes — a single couch or a marketplace pick-up is fine. The two-hour minimum still applies.

    Will you take pot plants?

    They ride up front with us, away from the heavy stuff. Water them the day before, not the morning of.

    Can you store things between moves?

    We don't run storage ourselves, but we'll happily deliver to, or collect from, a storage unit you've booked.

    What won't you move?

    Gas bottles, fuel, paint and anything flammable. Keep jewellery, cash and important papers with you.

    Is my stuff insured?

    Ask us about transit insurance when you book — we'll explain what's covered and what isn't, in plain English.
