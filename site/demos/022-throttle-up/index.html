/// Notice · 04

When it pours

Light rain

We ride. Wet-road practice is gold — drills slow down and we lend rain suits in sizes S to XXL.

Storm warning

A severe weather warning or lightning nearby and we stop. Your session moves to the next free weekend, no fee.

Heatwave

Summer sessions start at 6:30am and wrap by lunch, with a water station and a shade break every hour.

The callWe decide by 6am on the day and text everyone who’s booked. Check your phone before you leave home.
