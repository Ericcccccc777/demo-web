Surf safe

Flags, rips & you

We run through this on the sand before every first lesson. Here's the short version.

  1. Surf between the red and yellow flags

    That's the patrolled area where lifeguards are watching. Our lessons set up inside it or right beside it.

  2. Learn to spot a rip

    Look for darker, deeper-looking water with fewer breaking waves, a rippled surface, or foam and sand drifting out to sea.

  3. Caught in one? Relax and float

    Raise an arm and call for help. You may be able to escape by swimming parallel to the beach, towards the breaking waves.

  4. Know your limits

    Never surf alone, check the conditions and warnings first, and ask a lifeguard if you're unsure.

General information only. Conditions change quickly — always follow lifeguards' directions and official warnings. In an emergency, call Triple Zero (000).
