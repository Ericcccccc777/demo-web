Materials

What’s on the table

Aprons, easels and a sink big enough for everyone. Tea and biscuits at every session.

We supply

  • Easels, boards and drawing horses
  • Cartridge and watercolour paper
  • Charcoal, conté and pastels
  • Clay, glazes and two firings
  • Aprons, brushes and solvents

You bring

  • An old shirt you don’t mind ruining
  • A sketchbook, if you have one
  • Oil paints for Wednesdays (starter kit $85)
  • Closed shoes for the ceramics room
  • Nothing else. Really.
