Your first class

What to expect when you walk in

  1. Arrive ten minutes early. The door opens fifteen minutes before class, and the kettle's usually on.

  2. Leave your mat at home. Mats, blocks, bolsters and blankets are all here. Bring water and a layer for the end.

  3. Tell your teacher what's going on. Injuries, pregnancy, a rough week — quietly, before class, is perfect.

  4. Take the easier version. Every pose has one, and resting in child's pose is always allowed.

  5. No mirrors, no phones, no audience. Nobody is watching you — they're all busy breathing.

General information only. Yoga here is a wellbeing practice, not medical advice or treatment. If you have an injury or health condition, check with your GP or physio before you start.
