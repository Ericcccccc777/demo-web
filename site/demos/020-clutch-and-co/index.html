Why learn in a manual?

  • Drive anythingUtes, vans, older cars, the hire car on holiday — with a clutch or without, you’re covered.
  • Check the licence rulesIn some states, passing your practical test in an automatic can limit you to automatics for a while. Check South Australia’s current rules before you book a test.
  • Clutch control firstLesson one happens in a quiet car park: friction point, smooth take-offs, stalls that don’t matter.
  • Hills when you’re readyThe foothills are perfect hill-start practice. We build up to them — nobody gets thrown in.
