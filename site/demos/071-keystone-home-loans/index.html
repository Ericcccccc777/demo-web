How brokers are paid

Straight answer: usually by the lender.

For most home loans the lender you choose pays us, not you. It generally comes in two parts:

Upfront commission
A one-off payment when your loan settles — typically around 0.5–0.7% of the amount borrowed, less any money sitting in an offset account.
Trail commission
A smaller ongoing payment — typically around 0.1–0.2% a year of what you still owe. It stops when the loan is repaid or refinanced.

Rates differ between lenders, which is why brokers must act in your best interests: commission can’t be the reason for a recommendation. Before you apply you’ll get our credit guide and a written proposal showing what the lender would pay us on your loan.
