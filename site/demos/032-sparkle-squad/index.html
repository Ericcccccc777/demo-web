    FAQ

    Good questions, straight answers.

    Do I need to be home?

    No. Most clients leave a key in a lockbox or give us a door code. We text when we arrive and again when we lock up.

    Do you bring your own products and equipment?

    Yes — vacuum, mops, microfibre cloths and low-fragrance, plant-based products. If you'd rather we used yours, just leave them out and say so.

    What if something's missed?

    Tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and redo that spot at no charge.

    Can I skip or pause a regular clean?

    Yes. Give us 48 hours' notice and there's no fee — handy for holidays or when the in-laws are visiting.

    Is the online price final?

    It's an illustrative estimate. We confirm the actual price after a short video walkthrough or a few photos, before any work is booked.

    Do you clean carpets?

    We vacuum them every visit. Steam cleaning needs different equipment, so we quote it separately.

    Rather talk to a person?

    Call or email and we'll sort out a quick walkthrough. We answer between cleans, Monday to Saturday.
