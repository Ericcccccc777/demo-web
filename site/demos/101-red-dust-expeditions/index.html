    Before you go

    After dark, it gets better

    Out here the Milky Way throws shadows. Every camp is chosen for a low horizon and no lights for kilometres, so star-gazing is part of every night — telescope, red torches and a guide who knows the southern sky.

    Questions we get

    If yours isn’t here, call the office or send us a note from the booking form — a real person answers.

    Do I need 4WD experience?

    No. Your guide does all the driving. You ride, spot wildlife, and maybe help boil the billy.

    Is there phone reception?

    Only close to town. Every vehicle carries a satellite phone and an emergency beacon, and we check in with the office each evening.

    What are the toilets like?

    Mostly bush toilets and a portable loo with a very good view. Station Camp on Day 4 has hot showers.

    Can you cater for dietary needs?

    Yes — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies, as long as you tell us when you book.

    What if it rains or a track closes?

    It’s rare, but it happens. We re-route on open, safe tracks. If a tour can’t run, you choose a new date or a refund (sample policy).

    Our tours travel on the lands of the Traditional Owners of Central Australia. We follow park rules and signage, keep to open tracks, and respect areas that are closed to visitors.
