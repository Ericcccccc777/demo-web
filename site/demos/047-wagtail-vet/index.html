        Signs that can't wait until morning

        • Trouble breathing or blue-grey gums
        • Collapse, seizures or can't stand
        • Swollen, tight belly or retching with nothing coming up
        • Possible poisoning, snake bite or tick paralysis
        • Heavy bleeding or a major injury
        • Rabbits: not eating or no droppings for 12 hours

        General information only. If you're unsure, call — it's always OK to ask.
