    FAQ

    Good questions

    Do I need a trial?

    Not always, but most brides like one. It’s where we settle the look, test how it wears and take photos in daylight.

    I have sensitive skin. Is that okay?

    Let me know when you enquire. I can patch test at your trial and I’m happy to work with products you already know suit you.

    How long does it take?

    Around 75 minutes for the bride and 40 minutes for each person in the bridal party. I’ll build a timeline with you.

    Can you work with my traditions?

    Yes. Tell me about the ceremony, the outfits and any changes through the day, and I’ll plan the looks — and the timing — around them.

    How far ahead should I book?

    Popular dates go early, so enquire as soon as you have yours. A deposit holds the date once you’re happy with the quote.
