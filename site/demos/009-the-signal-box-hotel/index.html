    The beer garden

    A brick-walled courtyard behind the old goods shed, strung with festoon lights and shaded by a grapevine that's older than anyone can prove. Heaters come out in May.

    • Warm all winterGas heaters and fleece blankets from May to September.
    • Dogs welcomeOn a lead, with water bowls by the gate and a jar of treats at the bar.
    • Kids' cornerA chalk wall, a box of quoits and a clear line of sight from the tables.
    • Groups up to 60Birthdays and wakes alike — ask about platters and a bar tab.

    Trivia, a taste

    Three questions about our own (slightly tall) history. Answer on the beer mats — they flip to tell you how you went.

    1. 1

      Where does the pub get its name?

      A signal box stood across the tracks until the 1960s. Its brass lever plates now hang above the front bar.

    2. 2

      Which regular had her own spot on the front bar?

      Mrs Pickles the cockatoo, who — so the story goes — called last drinks at ten to eleven, sharp.

    3. 3

      About how many steps from platform one to the front bar?

      Roughly 380 at a stroll — about four minutes, or three if you're chasing last orders.

    Getting here

    We're the green-tiled corner building directly opposite Bendigo Station — drawn here the way the old signalmen would have.

    1. Leave by the main exitCome off the train, walk the length of platform one and out onto the forecourt.
    2. Cross at the lightsIt's the only set of lights on the street. You'll already see the gold lettering.
    3. Front door under the fasciaStep-free entry is through the beer-garden gate on the laneway, with an accessible toilet on the ground floor.
    4. Driving?Twelve free spaces behind the pub, off the laneway. Street parking is free after 6pm.
