    Before your appointment

    A few small things make bath day calmer for everyone.

    1. Walk firstA quick loo stop and a sniffy walk helps your dog settle on the table.
    2. Tell us the tricky bitsMatting, itchy skin, sore hips or worries — mention them when you book.
    3. Vaccinations up to dateWe ask every dog to be current, for their safety and their friends'.
    4. Light breakfastA smaller meal on the day is kinder before a warm bath.
    5. We'll text youAbout 15 minutes before your dog is ready to be picked up.

    General information only — we're groomers, not vets. If you notice skin, ear or eye problems, please see your vet before booking.
