Service manualSection 2 — Logbook servicing

§ 2

Logbook servicing

Your logbook sets the schedule — usually every 10,000–15,000 km or 6–12 months, whichever comes first. We follow it line by line, fit parts that meet the maker's spec, and stamp the book so the history stays complete.

TipUnder new-car warranty? Bring the logbook along. Your warranty booklet explains its own conditions — we follow the manufacturer's schedule and specs.

Service manualSection 3 — Tyres & brakes

§ 3

Tyres & brakes

The two things between you and the road. Here's what we measure, and the signs that mean it's time to book.

Book a brake check if…

  • you hear a squeal or a grind when braking
  • the pedal feels soft, or sinks further than it used to
  • the car pulls to one side as you stop
  • you feel shudder through the pedal or the wheel
  • the brake warning light comes on
