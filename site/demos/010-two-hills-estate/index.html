      The cellar door

      An old hay shed with a long verandah

      Our cellar door sits at the top of the gravel drive, looking straight between the two hills and out toward the gulf. Bring the dog, take the good chair and give yourself time — most people stay for a second glass.

      • Dogs on leads are welcome on the lawn
      • Step-free path from the car park to the bar
      • Kids welcome — there’s a sandpit under the lemon tree
      • Designated drivers get a free house lemonade

      Getting here

      McLaren Vale, South Australia — about 45 minutes’ drive south of Adelaide. Park on the gravel at the top of the drive; there are two accessible bays by the door. Minibus and tour groups, please call ahead.
