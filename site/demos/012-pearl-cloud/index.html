    Good to know

    常见问题
    How sweet is 50%?

    Half of our standard syrup. Most regulars land on 25–50%. Brown sugar drinks carry their own syrup, so 0% still tastes sweet.

    Can I swap to oat milk?

    Yes, for +$0.80 (sample price). Ask at the counter when you pick up; this demo builder uses our standard fresh milk.

    What are the pearls made of?

    Tapioca starch, water and brown sugar, cooked in small batches through the day so they stay soft and chewy.

    Allergens?

    Our drinks can contain milk, soy, egg (pudding) and gluten, and everything is made on shared equipment. Please ask staff before ordering if you have an allergy.

    Group or party orders?

    For 10+ cups, email hello@example.com a day ahead and we’ll have them sealed and boxed.
