How it works

From a chat to a live site in four steps.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your business

    Tell us what you do, who your customers are and what they should be able to do on your website. Send an enquiry or email us directly.

  2. 2

    Pick a few drawers

    Save the designs that feel right. They help us understand your taste without needing design vocabulary.

  3. 3

    See it, shape it

    We design with your real words and photos, then share a working preview you can click through on your phone. You tell us what to change.

  4. 4

    Launch & look after

    We set up your domain and hosting in your name, test on real devices, and show you how to make simple updates.

Timelines and pricing depend on scope — you'll get a written quote before any work starts.

Every site, as standard

The unglamorous things, done properly.

  • Designed for phones first

    Most customers will find you on a phone. Every layout is designed for small screens, not squeezed onto them.

  • Fast by default

    Lightweight pages, appropriately sized images and carefully chosen features help your customers get where they need to go.

  • Easy to read, easy to use

    Readable contrast, keyboard access and a sensible structure — the basics of accessibility, built in.

  • Ready for search

    Proper titles, descriptions and page structure so search engines understand your business. Nobody can promise rankings — we won't either.

  • A clear handover

    Domain and service accounts should be in your name. Rights in custom work, handover files and third-party licences are set out in your agreement.

  • Clear scope and pricing

    Know what is included, what it costs and what happens after launch. Any additional work is agreed before it begins.

FAQ

Good questions.

Ask us something else →
Are the businesses in the box real?

They are original concepts for fictional businesses, created to show different styles and interactions. Your website is built around your real brand, services and customers.

Can a design here be the starting point for my website?

Yes — any drawer can be a starting point. We adapt the layout and style to your brand, your words and your photos, so it ends up yours rather than a copy.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted to its scope and your budget. We explain the deliverables, payment stages and ongoing costs in writing before work starts. Fair pricing, with changes agreed before additional work begins.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. We ask plain questions about your business. You bring your logo if you have one, photos, services and prices — we handle the rest.

Can I update the site myself?

If you'd like to. For businesses that change menus or prices often we set up a simple way to edit them; otherwise we can make updates for you. We'll recommend what suits you.

Do you work outside my city?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses across Australia and internationally, using email and scheduled video calls.

Can you build in English and Chinese?

Yes. We write each language for its readers rather than translating word for word, and can add other languages too.

What should I prepare?

Start with a goal. Your current website, brand materials, service information, references and budget range help us propose a sensible scope. Let us know if you need help with copy or images.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the pages, features, content readiness and review schedule. We include estimated milestones in your proposal after scoping. Tell us early if you have a fixed launch date.

Are there ongoing costs?

Domains, hosting, paid tools and maintenance may have ongoing fees. Your quote explains what is included, what you pay directly and how future updates work. Sending an enquiry is free.
