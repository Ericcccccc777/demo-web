Web design in Sydney: custom websites for small businesses

We design and develop custom websites for Sydney small businesses, from a basic one-page site to multi-page websites with bookings, menus or ordering. Basic one-page websites for individuals and sole traders start from A$300; larger websites, apps and business tools are quoted in writing. Meet us in Sydney or work with us online, in English or Chinese.

Who this is for

  • Sydney sole traders and small businesses that need a website explaining their services, prices and how to book or enquire.
  • Restaurants, schools, clinics and service businesses whose customers need the same information in English and Chinese.
  • Owners who want to start with a basic one-page website and add pages, bookings or a second language later.

The scope

  • The basic one-page scope

    • One scrollable page with up to six content sections. Agree the sections around the information your customers need before design starts.
    • Layouts for phones, tablets and computers, so the same business information remains readable at each screen size.
    • Contact options: an enquiry form, or phone, email or WeChat buttons, selected around how you want customers to reach you.
    • Basic search settings: a page title, description and sharing image. These explain the page without promising a search position.
    • Launch on a domain and hosting account in your name. Account access and handover arrangements are agreed with you.
    • One round of revisions within the agreed scope. Bring the requested changes together so the review can address them clearly.

  • Quoted separately

    • Domain registration and hosting fees are separate from the website build.
    • Copywriting and photography are not included in the basic scope; agree who prepares the text and images.
    • A second language is a separate scope, including the matching pages and their content.
    • Booking or payment connections are additional work; third-party platform fees are explained beforehand.
    • Long-term maintenance is not part of the basic build and needs an agreed arrangement or separate quote.

Meet in Sydney, or work online

If you'd prefer to talk face to face, we can arrange a meeting in Sydney to go through your business, your customers and the pages you need. Projects can also run entirely online, by email and scheduled video calls. Either way, you talk directly with the people who design and develop your website, in English or Chinese.

Meetings are arranged in Sydney time, and you review layouts and working previews at agreed milestones before launch.

Bilingual menus, bookings and enquiries

For restaurants, tutoring schools and clinics, customers often need the same practical information in English and Chinese: menus, prices, lesson or appointment times, and how to book. We organise that information so both audiences can act on it.

Booking or ordering connections use an established service you choose. Setup work and platform fees are explained before you decide.

Start with one page, then grow

A basic one-page website can be a sensible first step: one scrollable page with up to six sections that explains what you do and how to reach you.

When you need more, we scope additional pages, a second language or a booking connection as separate work, so you only add what your business uses.
