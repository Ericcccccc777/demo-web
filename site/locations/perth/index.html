Web design in Perth: custom websites for small businesses

We design and develop custom websites for small businesses in Perth, Western Australia, from a basic one-page site to multi-page websites with quote requests, bookings or a small online shop. Basic one-page websites for individuals and sole traders start from A$300; larger websites, apps and business tools are quoted in writing. Meet us in Perth or work with us online, in English or Chinese.

Who this is for

  • Perth sole traders and tradies who need a clear website for their services, service area and quote requests.
  • Clinics, studios and shops that want bookings, enquiries or a small online shop connected to an established service.
  • Businesses with English- and Chinese-speaking customers who want both languages kept consistent.

The scope

  • The basic one-page scope

    • One scrollable page with up to six content sections. Agree the sections around the information your customers need before design starts.
    • Layouts for phones, tablets and computers, so the same business information remains readable at each screen size.
    • Contact options: an enquiry form, or phone, email or WeChat buttons, selected around how you want customers to reach you.
    • Basic search settings: a page title, description and sharing image. These explain the page without promising a search position.
    • Launch on a domain and hosting account in your name. Account access and handover arrangements are agreed with you.
    • One round of revisions within the agreed scope. Bring the requested changes together so the review can address them clearly.

  • Quoted separately

    • Domain registration and hosting fees are separate from the website build.
    • Copywriting and photography are not included in the basic scope; agree who prepares the text and images.
    • A second language is a separate scope, including the matching pages and their content.
    • Booking or payment connections are additional work; third-party platform fees are explained beforehand.
    • Long-term maintenance is not part of the basic build and needs an agreed arrangement or separate quote.

Meet in Perth, or work online

If you'd prefer to talk face to face, we can arrange a meeting in Perth to discuss your business and the pages you need. Projects can also run entirely online, by email and scheduled video calls. Either way, you talk directly with the people who design and develop your website, in English or Chinese.

Meetings are arranged in Perth time (AWST). You review layouts and working previews at agreed milestones before launch.

Service areas and quote requests

For tradies and home-service businesses, a useful website states the jobs you take, the suburbs you actually serve and how a customer can request a quote, with a click-to-call button for people on their phones.

A quote form or online calculator is scoped separately and built around your real pricing rules. Licence and insurance details appear only when you hold them and confirm the wording.

Bookings, online shops and ecommerce

Clinics and studios can link to an established booking service, and small retailers can connect a hosted ecommerce platform or checkout for a small online shop. We assess the service you use or prefer before recommending a connection.

Setup work, subscriptions and payment-related fees are explained before you decide, and they are kept separate from the website build.
