EMVALUE / Industry websites

Websites for salons and beauty studios

We design websites for hair, nail, brow and beauty studios in Australia, with clear service information, prices and enquiries. We can assess connections to your existing booking tool and quote bilingual pages in writing before work starts. We mainly work online, with direct communication in English and Chinese.

Who this is for

  • Studios that need a clear service and price list beyond their latest social post.
  • Owners who want customers to find their existing booking route, location and opening hours in one place.
  • Businesses serving English- and Chinese-speaking clients and considering a bilingual website.

The scope

  • What we agree in the scope

    • An agreed structure for service descriptions, price lists and the work images you provide.
    • Assessment of your current booking tool and an explanation of connection scope and platform fees.
    • Location, opening hours, contact details and your real Instagram and Xiaohongshu (RedNote) profile links.
    • A written decision on language versions and how you will maintain prices after handover.

  • Scope separately

    • Basic one-page pricing does not automatically include photography, new copy or a second language.
    • Third-party booking subscriptions or ongoing updates unless agreed in the scope.
    • We do not invent customer images, social accounts, prices or booking availability.

Make prices and booking steps clear

Group services in terms customers recognise, and explain the difference between a listed price and a price that depends on a consultation. Use the service names and prices you confirm. If an add-on or choice changes the amount, make that distinction visible so the page is useful before a visitor starts a booking.

Tell us which booking tool you currently use. We can assess whether to link to it or connect it more closely, based on the platform and access available. Platform fees and connection work are explained before agreement. Your live booking system determines availability; a website enquiry or concept interaction is not a promise of an appointment.

Show your work to both audiences

Use genuine work photographs you are able to publish, with service labels that help customers understand what they are viewing. Choose images to explain your work, rather than treating a concept design as a real client result. Photography and copy preparation can be discussed separately from the website build.

For a bilingual site, keep service names, prices and booking instructions aligned between English and Chinese. Visitors should be able to reach the corresponding language page. Decide who supplies and approves the wording, and how later changes will update both versions. An extra language has its own scope; it is not automatically included in a one-page starting price.

Bring location and social profiles together

Provide the location and hours you actually use, and explain how customers should contact you before visiting. We can include your real Instagram and Xiaohongshu profile links so visitors can move between the website and your own work updates. Confirm the account addresses and information before publication; the website should not invent an address or social presence.

Questions before you start

Can it connect to my current booking system?

First tell us which system you use. We assess the platform, access and desired booking flow before confirming a connection. The quote describes the work and any platform fees; we do not assume every service supports the same integration.

Can I update the price list myself?

We discuss that requirement when choosing the platform and handover scope. Agree which prices you need to edit and whether the site has a managed editing route or needs an update service. The proposal records the arrangement, including how bilingual changes are kept aligned.

Do I need a Chinese version?

Consider whether your customers need Chinese service descriptions and booking guidance. A bilingual site can organise that information alongside English. It needs wording you can approve and a plan for keeping prices and instructions consistent; we scope the extra version separately.

Discuss the work you need

Send your current service and price list, booking platform, work images, location and hours. Tell us which languages your clients use. We can discuss a clear page structure, assess the booking route and prepare a written proposal.
