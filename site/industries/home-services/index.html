EMVALUE / Industry websites

Websites for tradies and home-service businesses

We design websites for tradies, cleaners, gardeners, removalists and other home-service businesses in Australia. We organise the service information and enquiry flow, then quote the agreed pages and functions in writing before work starts. We mainly work online, with direct communication in English and Chinese.

Who this is for

  • Sole traders who need a clear place to explain their services and receive enquiries from a phone.
  • Small teams whose customers need to check service areas, job types and the next step before asking for a quote.
  • Businesses considering a quote form, calculator or appointment request alongside their service information.

The scope

  • What we agree in the scope

    • An agreed page structure for services, actual service areas, enquiries and work photographs you provide.
    • Clear mobile contact actions and the fields needed to understand a job before you respond.
    • Your real licences and insurance information, only where you supply and confirm it for publication.
    • A written decision on which enquiry or appointment-request functions belong in the quoted scope.

  • Scope separately

    • Photography, new copy and additional languages unless included in the written quote.
    • Basic one-page pricing does not automatically include a calculator, booking connection or ongoing maintenance.
    • We do not invent work photographs, qualifications, service areas or response-time promises.

Answer the questions before the call

Explain which jobs you take, where you travel and how a customer can request a quote. A visitor should be able to tell whether the service fits before sending a message. Separate the work you do from work that needs a different discussion, and describe any information needed to assess the request.

Set response expectations using your actual working arrangements. If you cannot promise an exact reply time, explain when messages are checked and which contact route to use. Work photographs should come from jobs you can genuinely show. Present licence and insurance details only when you actually hold them and have confirmed the wording.

Choose a useful enquiry flow

A quote form can collect the job type, location and details you need to review a request. A click-to-call button helps visitors reach the phone number you provide. An appointment request can ask for a preferred time, while making clear that a request is not a confirmed booking until you accept it.

For an online calculator, agree the inputs and pricing rules before development. Decide whether the result is an estimate or a quote you will honour, and what requires a site assessment. We can assess an existing booking connection. The written scope explains the chosen functions and any third-party platform costs rather than treating every feature as standard.

Questions before you start

What if I do not have work photos?

Tell us what you can provide. We can discuss a page structure that uses clear service descriptions while you prepare genuine images. Photography is a separate scope. The concepts on this page are fictional design examples, not substitutes for your own completed work.

Can you build online quotes?

We can assess a quote form or calculator against your actual pricing rules. Explain what changes the price and which jobs need inspection. We confirm the calculation, result wording and development scope before quoting; a concept calculator does not establish your business prices.

How should I describe service areas?

List the suburbs or regions you really serve and any boundaries customers need to know. Confirm the list before publication and explain when a job outside it needs a separate discussion. A design example’s location is not evidence of your coverage.

How much does the website cost?

Basic one-page websites for individuals and sole traders start from A$300. A calculator, additional pages or booking connection changes the scope. The website cost guide explains inclusions, exclusions and continuing costs; your written quote confirms the actual project before work starts.

Discuss the work you need

Send your service list, actual service areas, available photographs and the contact route you use. Tell us what you need to know before quoting a job. We can discuss the pages and enquiry flow and prepare a written scope.
